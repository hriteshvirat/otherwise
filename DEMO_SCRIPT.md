# OTHERWISE — 2-Minute Demo Video Script

> **Premise Hook**: *"What if you couldn't control the world... but you could control what it wanted?"*

---

## 🎬 Video Overview
- **Duration**: ~2 Minutes
- **Style**: Show-not-tell, fast-paced, high visual clarity
- **Target Audience**: Hackathon Judges & Indie Players

---

## ⏱️ Minute-by-Minute Breakdown

### 0:00 – 0:15 | The Opening & Premise
- **Visual**: Start on the title screen of **OTHERWISE** with drifting ambient particles and ambient synthesizer music.
- **Action**: Click `PLAY`.
- **Narration (or Text Card)**:
  > *"In traditional puzzle games, you give the player new powers or push predefined buttons. In **OTHERWISE**, you give objects desires. When you change what things want, the world changes how they behave."*

---

### 0:15 – 0:45 | Core Mechanic in Action (Level 1)
- **Visual**: The player creature approaches a narrow corridor blocked by a heavy boulder.
- **Action**:
  1. Open the Concept Wheel (`Q` or `Tab`).
  2. Select **FEAR**.
  3. Press `E` on the boulder.
- **Reaction**: The boulder pulses purple, shivers, spots the player, and rolls away down the corridor, opening the route.
- **Narration**:
  > *"Assign FEAR, and a stone perceives you as danger and flees."*

---

### 0:45 – 1:15 | Emergence & Multi-Concept Synergies (Levels 2–5)
- **Visual 1 (Level 2 - The Lonely Bridge)**:
  - Wide broken bridge gap.
  - Assign **LONELY** to scattered stones across the chasm — they seek each other, aggregate together, and form physical stepping stones.
- **Visual 2 (Hollow Woods - Level 7)**:
  - Deep emerald forest with hazards.
  - Assign **TRUST + PROTECTIVE** to a woodland creature. The creature shadows the player and body-blocks environmental hazards.
- **Narration**:
  > *"Assign LONELY, and stones seek companions to build bridges. Combine TRUST and PROTECTIVE, and a wild creature becomes your personal shield."*

---

### 1:15 – 1:45 | World Tour & The 13 Concepts
- **Visual**: Fast cinematic montage highlighting:
  - **Clockwork Ruins (Level 9)**: Assign **IMITATE** to automatons to synchronize dual pressure plates.
  - **Dreaming Mountains (Level 13)**: Floating celestial islands using **SLEEPY** stepping blocks and **REPEAT** ferry platforms.
- **Narration**:
  > *"Across 4 unique regions and 19 levels, discover 13 distinct Concepts — from GREED and JEALOUSY to IMITATION and SLEEP."*

---

### 1:45 – 2:05 | Creative Archive & Player Profiling
- **Visual**:
  1. Open the storybook **World Map** (`M`).
  2. Open the **Creative Archive** showcasing the player's emergent archetype (e.g. *THE ENGINEER*, *THE CHAOTIC*, *THE RULE BREAKER*) and the 6 dimension radar scores.
- **Narration**:
  > *"Every puzzle has multiple valid solutions. The Creative Archive tracks your experimentation and profiles your creative archetype."*

---

### 2:05 – 2:20 | Call to Action & End Card
- **Visual**: Triumphant jump into the Level 16 beacon portal with bursting particles.
- **End Card**:
  ```text
  OTHERWISE
  "There is always another way."
  ```
